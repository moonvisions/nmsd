IN-GAME PURCHASES ON STEAM, GOOGLE PLAY AND THE APP STORE (Round 69)
=====================================================================
Steam, Google and Apple require their OWN payments inside their versions of the game (Stripe stays
for the website). Your server checks every purchase with the store, and adds each one only once.

STEP 0 - UPDATE THE SERVER
 1. GitHub > your server repository > "Add file" > "Upload files".
 2. Drag in: storeverify.js, store.js, README.txt and this file. Click "Commit changes".
 3. Render > your service > Environment: for Big Night passes add BIGNIGHT_MODE = paid
 4. After adding each setting below, click "Save Changes" (Render restarts by itself).

STEP 1 - STEAM
 1. partner.steamgames.com > Users & Permissions > Manage Groups > your publisher group.
 2. Find "Web API Key" (click "Create WebAPI Key" if there isn't one). Copy it - it's a secret.
 3. On Render add:  STEAM_WEB_API_KEY = (the key)     STEAM_APP_ID = (your App ID number)
 4. Nothing to create in Steamworks: the server tells Steam the item and price (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD
    or AUD - whatever the player's Steam wallet uses).
 TEST WITHOUT REAL MONEY:
 5. Add STEAM_SANDBOX = 1 AND ALLOW_STEAM_SANDBOX_IN_PROD = 1 (without the second one, the live
    server switches Steam purchases OFF - so you can't forget test mode on).
 6. Start the game FROM STEAM, sign in, Store > Buy, approve the Steam box. "(test purchase)" appears.
 7. DELETE both settings when done.

STEP 2 - GOOGLE PLAY
 1. Play Console > your app > Monetize > Products > In-app products: "Create product" for EACH id
    above, with its price. Click "Activate".
 2. console.cloud.google.com: pick or create a project. Search "Google Play Android Developer API" > "Enable".
 3. Menu > IAM & Admin > Service Accounts > "Create service account" ("newmoon-purchases") > "Done".
 4. Click it > "Keys" > "Add key" > "Create new key" > JSON > "Create". A .json file downloads.
 5. Copy the service account's email (ends in .iam.gserviceaccount.com).
 6. Play Console (main page) > Users and permissions > "Invite new users": paste the email. Under
    "App permissions" add your app, tick "View financial data, orders, and cancellation survey
    responses" and "Manage orders and subscriptions". Click "Invite user".
 7. Open the .json in Notepad/TextEdit, copy ALL of it. On Render: GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON = (paste)
 8. /admin should say Google Play "ready" (permissions can take up to 24 hours).
 REFUNDS ARRIVE BY THEMSELVES (recommended):
 9. Make up a long password (20+ letters/numbers). On Render: GOOGLE_RTDN_SECRET = (it)
10. Google Cloud > Pub/Sub > Topics > "Create topic" ("play-notifications"). Open it > Permissions >
    "Add principal": google-play-developer-notifications@system.gserviceaccount.com, role "Pub/Sub Publisher".
11. In the topic: "Create subscription" > Delivery type "Push" > Endpoint:
    https://server.newmoonsamedark.com/store/google/rtdn?secret=(your password) > "Create".
12. Play Console > your app > Monetize > Monetization setup > Real-time developer notifications:
    paste the topic name (projects/.../topics/play-notifications) > "Send test notification" > Save.
 TEST WITHOUT REAL MONEY:
13. Play Console > Settings > License testing: add your Gmail. Install from an internal testing track.
    Purchases are free and marked TEST on /admin.

STEP 3 - APPLE APP STORE
 1. App Store Connect > your app > Monetization > In-App Purchases > "+": type CONSUMABLE, the EXACT
    product id, price, display name, review screenshot. Repeat for each product.
 2. Users and Access > Integrations > In-App Purchase > "Generate In-App Purchase Key".
    Download the .p8 (only ONCE). Note its "Key ID" and the "Issuer ID" at the top.
 3. On Render add:  APPLE_ISSUER_ID   APPLE_KEY_ID   APPLE_PRIVATE_KEY = (all of the .p8, from Notepad)
 4. Add APPLE_ALLOW_SANDBOX = 1 and KEEP it: Apple's reviewers buy in the free "sandbox".
    Sandbox purchases are marked TEST on /admin, and one button takes them all back.
 5. REFUNDS BY THEMSELVES: your app > App Information > App Store Server Notifications: Production
    AND Sandbox URL = https://server.newmoonsamedark.com/store/apple/notify  Version 2. Save.
 TEST WITHOUT REAL MONEY:
 6. Users and Access > Sandbox > Testers > "+": make a test Apple account.
 7. iPhone: Settings > App Store > Sandbox Account: sign in with it. Buy in the TestFlight build
    (free). Try "Restore purchases" too.

STEP 4 - THE GAME (Unity)
 1. Window > Package Manager > "+" > "Add package by name". Name: com.unity.purchasing
    Version: 4.15.1 (exactly - Google requires its Billing 8; NOT 5.x). Click "Add".
 2. Steam builds need Steamworks.NET; a Steam build without it is stopped (it would use Stripe).

REFUNDS AND PROBLEMS
 - Refunds are taken back automatically (notifications, plus a check every 6 hours). /admin also has
   "Check ... for refunds now" and a "Revoke" button per purchase.
 - A red "Purchases need your attention" box on /admin = a setting is wrong (package name, bundle id,
   key). Those purchases are KEPT and added once you fix it.
