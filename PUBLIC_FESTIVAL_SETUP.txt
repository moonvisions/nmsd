THE PUBLIC FESTIVAL - SETUP (Round 66)
One festival that is always open. Anyone joins or leaves any time, and nobody hosts it.

STEP 1 - UPDATE THE SERVER
 1. Go to github.com and open your repository "festival-tycoon-relay".
 2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
 3. From the game's "server" folder, drag in: relay.js, patchbay.js, payments.js, applications.js,
    README.txt, PUBLIC_FESTIVAL_SETUP.txt
 4. Click "Commit changes". Render restarts the server by itself. Wait 2-5 minutes until it says "Live".
 5. Check it: open https://YOUR-SERVER.onrender.com/healthz in your browser. It should say: ok

STEP 2 - PICK HOW IT STAYS ON
 Option A - free ($0)
  The free server sleeps after 15 quiet minutes. A free "pinger" keeps it awake:
  1. Make a free account at uptimerobot.com.
  2. "Add New Monitor" > Type: HTTP(s) > URL: https://YOUR-SERVER.onrender.com/healthz
     > Interval: 5 minutes > "Create".
  Good to know:
  - When you update the server (or Render restarts it), players are kicked out for a few seconds.
    Their games reconnect by themselves.
  - A restart forgets the DJ schedule. After changing it, press "Download backup" on the DJ page,
    then render.com > your service > "Environment" > "Secret Files" > add a file named
    patchbay.json and paste the whole backup in. (README.txt, "KEEPING THE PATCH BAY".)

 Option B - about $7 a month (rock solid)
  1. render.com > your service > "Settings" > Instance Type: "Starter" > Save. It never sleeps.
  2. "Disks" > "Add Disk": Name store-data, Mount Path /var/data, Size 1 GB > Save.
  3. "Environment" > add STORE_DATA_DIR with the value /var/data.
  Now the DJ schedule saves itself. No pinger needed.

STEP 3 - SETTINGS (optional)
 render.com > your service > "Environment" > "Add Environment Variable", then "Save":
  PUBLIC_CAPACITY      most people inside at once (normally 60)
  PUBLIC_DAY_SECONDS   length of one festival day in seconds (normally 7200 = 2 hours)
  ADMIN_KEY            your admin password (needed for the DJ page; probably already set)

STEP 4 - ADD DJ SET TIMES
 1. Open https://YOUR-SERVER.onrender.com/admin/patch and type your ADMIN_KEY.
 2. Add your DJs at the bottom of the page (if they aren't there yet).
 3. Find the card "The public festival (always open, 24/7)".
 4. Click "+ Patch a DJ into this event". Pick a stage and a DJ.
 5. When: "At a set time" > pick the date and time > type the set length in minutes.
 6. Click "Patch DJ". The DJ goes live by themselves at that time, and the game's schedule shows it.

STEP 5 - ARTIST APPLICATIONS (Round 67)
 DJs, VJs and live acts can ask to perform:
  - in the game: Menu > "Perform here (DJ / VJ / artist)"
  - or on the web: https://YOUR-SERVER.onrender.com/apply
 Each one gets a private code to check on their application.
 1. Open https://YOUR-SERVER.onrender.com/admin/applications and type your ADMIN_KEY.
 2. Read each card (their links open in a new tab). Type a note if you like - they see it.
 3. Approve: they join the DJ roster, and their code now shows their private DJ page link.
    Then give them a set time (STEP 4). Decline: they see "not this time" and your note.
 Free server: applications are forgotten when it restarts - check them often, or add the
 $7 disk (STEP 2, option B).

HOW PLAYERS JOIN
 1. Once: Settings > Play with friends > "Paste server address".
 2. The game shows how many people are inside. Press join and walk in.
 3. Switch roles (festival-goer, DJ, vendor, medic, security, crew) any time while playing.
