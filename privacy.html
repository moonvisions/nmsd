New Moon, Same Dark: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

In short: New Moon, Same Dark is made by Moon Vision Media LLC. Your career is saved on your own device. If you leave "Share play stats" on, the game tells our server how you play (so we can make it better). If you make an account, play online or buy something, our server keeps what's needed for that. We never sell your information and there are no ads.

Play stats (you can switch these off)

Unless you turn off Settings > Game > Share play stats, the game sends our server:

  • a random number made for your copy of the game (not your device's own ID), and a new random number each time you play;
  • the game's version, your platform (Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone, web), where you got the game, your device model or graphics card, operating system and language, and a few settings (graphics level, phone controls);
  • how long you play, whether you're on the map or walking the festival, your Festival Pass level and career tier;
  • what happens in the game: festivals started and finished (crowd size, profit, happiness, grade), mini-games played, photos taken (not the photos), joining the Open Festival or friends' festivals, purchases;
  • error messages if the game crashes, with where in the game's code it happened (anything that looks like an email address, a password or sign-in key, and folder names that could contain your computer's user name are removed first);
  • whether you're signed in to an account (yes or no - never your name or account ID).

The server also sees your internet address when the game connects (every website does); it's used to stop abuse and isn't stored with your play stats. Turning play stats off stops them from then on. To have what was already sent deleted, email us (below).

Device check (only if you run it). Settings > Device check walks through the game's screens on your device and sends us a report: your device model, operating system, screen size, the game build, any layout problems it found, and a screenshot of each screen. Your username, email and character name are blacked out, other players' names on lists are blacked out, and no account or device ID is sent. Reports are kept only to fix the game (the newest 40) and are deleted after that; email us to have one deleted sooner.

Accounts and purchases

An account is optional. If you make one we keep the name and email address you give, your Moon Shards and what you own. Payments are handled by Stripe (website and PC download), Steam, Google Play or the App Store - we never see your card details; we receive a confirmation of what was bought and its price.

Playing online and voice chat

When you play with others, our server passes game messages and voice between players. Voice and chat are passed straight on and never recorded or kept. Your player name is shown to the other players. If players report someone, the server keeps the report (the reported player's name and the reason) so we can act on it. World leaderboards keep the board name you choose and your scores.

Who sees it

Only Moon Vision Media. Our server runs on Render (render.com) in the United States. We don't sell or share your information, except with the payment companies above for purchases, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Play stats are kept while the game is running as a service and for up to 13 months of daily totals; accounts until you ask us to delete them (purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires).

The game engine

The Windows, Mac and phone versions are built with the Unity game engine. We haven't turned on any Unity analytics or advertising, but the engine itself may send Unity basic technical information about your device, under Unity's own privacy policy.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can switch play stats off at any time in Settings. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights, and we'll honour them.
